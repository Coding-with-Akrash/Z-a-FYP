About BioSecure

Pioneering deepfake detection with ensemble AI

Our Mission

To combat synthetic media threats with accessible, accurate, and privacy-focused detection technology. We believe in a world where authenticity can be verified instantly.

Why Ensemble?

Single-model detection often fails against sophisticated deepfakes. Our ensemble approach combines multiple AI models, each trained on different features. This multi-layered strategy catches what individual models miss, delivering 97% accuracy.

Deep Learning Computer Vision Forensic Analysis

How It Works

1
Multi-Model Analysis

Three AI models (EfficientNet, ResNet, Feature Extractor) analyze content simultaneously, each looking for different types of manipulation artifacts.

2
Feature Extraction

Extracts biological signals, visual artifacts, frequency patterns, and texture features that are invisible to the human eye.

3
Ensemble Voting

Combines predictions using weighted voting, where models that agree receive higher confidence scores.
